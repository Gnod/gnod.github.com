DM ADVENTURE / PRIVACY

隐私政策

Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日 · iPhone 与 iPad 应用Effective October 10, 2026 · iPhone and iPad app

DM大冒险是一款离线单机游戏。游戏不收集个人数据，不追踪你，也不会把游戏进度或设置发送给开发者。

DM Adventure is an offline single-player game. The app does not collect personal data, track you, or send your progress or settings to the developer.

设备上的游戏数据

Game data on your device

游戏进度、收集记录、通关记录和设置只保存在应用的本地存储中，用于恢复进度与保留你的偏好。本游戏没有账号系统、开发者运营的云存档或跨设备同步服务。你可以在游戏设置中清空进度；删除应用会移除其本地数据。

Progress, collected coins, completion records, and settings are stored in the app's local storage to resume your adventure and remember your preferences. The game has no account system, developer-operated cloud saves, or cross-device sync service. You can reset progress in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data.

不收集、不追踪

No collection or tracking

游戏不含广告、应用内购买、分析 SDK 或第三方追踪工具，不请求相机、麦克风、照片、通讯录或定位权限，也不读取广告标识符。游戏本身不上传诊断、设备标识或游戏行为数据。

The game contains no ads, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location, and it does not read advertising identifiers. The app does not upload diagnostics, device identifiers, or gameplay activity.

联系支持时

When you contact support

如果你主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址，以及你选择提供的内容，例如设备型号、系统版本、截图或问题描述。这些信息仅用于回复和解决你的请求，不用于广告或出售给第三方。请不要发送密码、付款信息或不必要的个人资料。

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your device model, system version, screenshots, or a description of the problem. We use this information to respond to and resolve your request, not for advertising or sale to third parties. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

这个网站

This website

本网站提供游戏介绍、截图、支持和隐私信息，不包含可玩的游戏。页面不使用广告、分析脚本、追踪 Cookie 或外部字体。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；托管服务商可能为了提供和保护网站而处理 IP 地址等常规请求日志。打开邮件链接会使用你选择的邮件应用。

This website provides game information, screenshots, support, and privacy details. It does not host a playable game. The pages use no ads, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or external fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary request logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect its service. Opening an email link uses your chosen mail application.

儿童隐私

Children's privacy

游戏不会收集任何年龄玩家的个人数据。如果你认为在支持沟通过程中提供了儿童个人信息，请联系我们，以便处理删除请求。

The app does not collect personal data from players of any age. If you believe a child's personal information was provided in a support message, contact us so we can address a deletion request.
