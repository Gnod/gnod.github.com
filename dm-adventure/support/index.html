DM ADVENTURE / PLAYER SUPPORT

玩家支持

Player Support

简体中文 / English · iPhone 与 iPadEnglish / Simplified Chinese · iPhone and iPad

一款把成语变成机关的离线像素平台跳跃游戏。五个世界，33 个主线关卡，外加 5 个隐藏关。

An offline pixel platformer that turns Chinese idioms into surprising traps and puzzles. Explore five worlds, 33 main stages, and five secret stages.

触屏操作

Touch controls

  • 在左半屏按住并左右滑动来移动，手指越过中线会换方向；向下拉可以钻水管。
  • 点击右半屏进行小跳，按住可以跳得更高。
  • 屏幕顶部依次是暂停、重来、音乐和音效。暂停菜单可继续游戏、重来、进入设置或返回地图。
  • 在世界地图上拖动浏览，点关卡节点让主角走过去，再点右下角的开始按钮进入关卡。
  • Hold and slide on the left half of the screen to move. Crossing the center changes direction; pull downward to enter pipes.
  • Tap the right half for a short jump, or hold to jump higher.
  • The buttons at the top control pause, restart, music, and sound effects. The pause menu lets you resume, restart, open Settings, or return to the map.
  • Drag the world map to look around, tap a stage node to walk there, then tap Start in the lower-right corner.

金币与隐藏关

Coins and secret stages

碰到存档旗或完成关卡后，本次拿到的金币才会保存；在这之前失败，尚未保存的金币会回到关卡里。不同尝试中收集过的金币会累计记录。收集一个世界主线金币的七成，可开启该世界的隐藏关。完成第四世界的大结局，并累计收集 150 枚金币后，云上天宫开放；完成第五世界可看到真结局。

Newly collected coins are saved when you reach a checkpoint flag or finish a stage. If you fail before then, unsaved coins return to the stage. Coins collected across different attempts count toward your total. Collect 70% of a world's main-stage coins to open its secret stage. Finish the fourth world's ending and collect 150 coins in total to unlock the Sky Palace. Complete the fifth world to see the true ending.

进度与设置

Progress and settings

进度和设置自动保存在当前设备上。无需账号或联网，也没有跨设备云存档。删除应用会删除本地进度。设置中可以调整音乐和音效音量、震屏和手机震动，切换中文或英文，也可以清空进度；清空前需要再次确认，音量等偏好会保留。

Progress and settings are saved automatically on the current device. No account or internet connection is required, and there are no cross-device cloud saves. Deleting the app deletes local progress. Settings lets you adjust music, effects, screen shake, and haptics, switch between English and Simplified Chinese, or reset progress. Resetting requires a second confirmation and keeps preferences such as volume.

遇到问题

Troubleshooting

  • 关卡未解锁：先完成前一关；隐藏关和第五世界还需要达到金币条件。
  • 没有声音：检查游戏的音乐、音效按钮和设置中的音量，再检查设备音量与输出设备。
  • 游戏显示或操作异常：退出后重新打开应用，并确认设备运行 iOS / iPadOS 15.0 或更高版本。
  • 仍有问题：请邮件告知关卡名称、出现问题时的操作和复现步骤。
  • A stage is locked: complete the preceding stage first. Secret stages and the fifth world also have coin requirements.
  • No sound: check the game's music and effects buttons and volume settings, then check your device volume and audio output.
  • Display or control problems: close and reopen the app, and confirm your device runs iOS / iPadOS 15.0 or later.
  • If the issue continues, email the stage name, what you were doing, and the steps to reproduce it.
