LUMA / 隐私政策

创作留在你手中。

本政策适用于 Gnod Studio 提供的 Luma iOS 应用与本产品网站。生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日。

开发者不收集你的编辑素材

Luma 在设备上解码、预览、编辑和导出照片、RAW 文件与视频，不把这些素材、配方或编辑操作发送到 Gnod Studio 的服务器。应用没有账号、广告、行为分析或跟踪 SDK，也不进行跨应用跟踪。

照片权限与 iCloud

当你打开素材选择器时，Luma 通过 Apple PhotoKit 请求照片图库权限；你可以授予有限访问或完整访问。保存作品时会请求添加照片权限。你可以随时在 iOS 的“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片”中调整授权。文件导入器仅访问你选择的文件。

若素材存储在 iCloud 照片中，系统可能通过 Apple 服务获取缩略图或原始文件；保存到照片图库的作品也可能随你的 iCloud 设置同步。iCloud、设备备份及系统诊断由 Apple 与你的系统设置管理，不等于 Luma 向开发者上传素材。

配方与临时文件

你保存的配方名称与调整参数存于当前设备的应用存储空间（UserDefaults），用于再次应用或删除配方。导入与导出过程中可能在应用临时目录生成媒体副本；这些文件不会自动发送给开发者。Luma 不提供开发者运营的云同步。

管理与删除本地数据

你可以在“我的配方”中删除单条配方。若要移除应用容器内的配方与临时数据，请使用 iOS 的“删除 App”；“卸载 App”可能保留文稿和数据。重新安装或恢复设备备份可能恢复备份中的数据，备份由你在系统中管理。

已经保存到照片图库的导出作品独立于 App 保留；如需移除，请在“照片”中删除，并按需要清理“最近删除”。删除 App 不会自动删除原始照片或这些导出作品。

你主动联系支持时

如果你向 gnodstudio@gmail.com 发送邮件，我们会收到你主动提供的邮箱地址、正文与附件，仅用于处理咨询、定位问题和跟进回复。请勿发送密码、付款资料或不必要的私人照片。支持邮件由所用邮件服务处理；你可通过同一邮箱要求删除所提交的支持资料，法律或安全需要保留的记录除外。

本网站

本网站仅提供产品介绍、界面截图、帮助和隐私说明。我们不设置广告、分析追踪、跟踪 Cookie 或收集资料的表单。语言选择通过网址参数传递，不需要 Cookie 或浏览器本地存储。

网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。托管方可能处理 IP 地址、请求时间等提供网站及保障安全所需的技术日志，具体以 GitHub 隐私声明为准。

儿童、联系与更新

Luma 不以收集任何用户的个人数据为目的，也不主动收集儿童的个人数据。如对隐私有疑问，或希望处理主动提交给支持的资料，请联系 gnodstudio@gmail.com。政策有变化时，我们会更新本页的生效日期。

LUMA / Privacy policy

Your work stays yours.

This policy covers the Luma iOS app from Gnod Studio and this product website. Effective October 28, 2026.

Your editing media is not collected by the developer

Luma decodes, previews, edits, and exports photos, RAW files, and videos on your device. It does not send that media, recipes, or editing activity to Gnod Studio servers. The app has no accounts, ads, behavioral analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps.

Photo permissions and iCloud

When you open the media picker, Luma requests photo-library access through Apple PhotoKit. You may grant limited or full access. Saving an export requires permission to add to Photos. You can change access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. The file importer accesses only files you choose.

For media stored in iCloud Photos, the system may retrieve thumbnails or originals through Apple services. Exports saved to Photos may also sync according to your iCloud settings. Apple and your system settings manage iCloud, device backups, and system diagnostics; these are separate from uploading media to the developer.

Recipes and temporary files

Saved recipe names and adjustment values are stored in the app’s local storage (UserDefaults), so you can reuse or delete recipes. Importing and exporting may create media copies in the app’s temporary directory. These are not automatically sent to the developer. Luma does not provide a developer-operated cloud-sync service.

Manage or delete local data

You can delete individual recipes in My Recipes. To remove recipes and temporary data in the app container, use Delete App in iOS; Offload App may retain documents and data. Reinstalling or restoring a device backup may restore backed-up data. You manage backups in system settings.

Exports already saved to Photos remain separate from the app. To remove them, delete them in Photos and, if desired, clear Recently Deleted. Deleting the app does not automatically remove your original photos or these exports.

When you contact support

If you email gnodstudio@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, investigate the issue, and follow up. Do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal photos. The email provider processes support mail. You can use the same address to request deletion of submitted support information, except records that must be retained for legal or security purposes.

This website

This website provides product information, interface screenshots, help, and privacy information. We do not add advertising, analytics tracking, tracking cookies, or forms that collect personal information. Language choices use URL parameters, without cookies or browser local storage.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. The host may process IP addresses, request times, and other technical logs needed to operate and secure the website. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Children, contact, and updates

Luma is not designed to collect personal data from any user and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. For privacy questions or requests about information sent to support, email gnodstudio@gmail.com. We will update the effective date when this policy changes.
