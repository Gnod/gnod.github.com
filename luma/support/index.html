LUMA / 帮助与支持

继续你的创作。

Luma 支持 iOS 17 或更新版本。应用正在准备上架；本页不提供 App Store 下载或公开测试邀请。

从素材到作品

导入照片或 RAW 后，打开“预设”，通过实时缩略图选择效果并调整滤镜强度。顶部“撤销”可回退最近的编辑；按住预览可以对照原图。喜欢当前效果时，可打开“配方”保存，供以后或批量处理时使用。

裁剪与 HSL 调整

打开“裁剪”后，可在预览上拖动裁剪框及其控制点来调整取景，并结合比例、旋转、翻转和透视工具完成构图。在“HSL”中先选颜色通道，再选色相、饱和度或明度；“重置通道”只重置当前选中的颜色通道。

在 iPhone Duo 的展开屏幕上，基础调整按组排列，HSL 的八个颜色通道以网格展示，配方则显示为纵向列表。

照片权限与找不到素材

在系统“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → Luma”中检查权限。有限访问时，应用只能显示你允许访问的素材；可在系统中调整选中的照片。

若素材只在 iCloud 中，请先在“照片”中打开并等待下载完成，再返回 Luma 导入。当前选择器最多展示最近的 120 项匹配媒体；较旧素材可先另存到“文件”，再使用文件导入入口（照片和 RAW）。

RAW 和视频兼容性

应用支持常见照片、DNG RAW 与 MP4 视频，具体相机 RAW、编解码器和视频规格取决于设备与系统解码能力。RAW 编辑使用轻量预览，导出时重新解码原始文件；导出的是编辑后的图像，不是重新写入 RAW 原片。

大型 RAW、长视频或高分辨率视频会需要更多内存、存储空间与处理时间。导出时请保留足够可用空间，尽量让应用保持前台。如果某个文件无法导入，可用另一个同类型文件排查，并联系支持提供文件格式和设备信息。

导出与预览

请检查 Luma 是否有“添加照片”权限，再点“导出”。照片保存成功后，提示框提供“分享”和“继续编辑”：点“分享”会打开系统分享菜单，由你选择接收照片的 App 或联系人。原始素材不会被覆盖。

视频编辑中可设置入点、出点、速度、静音、滤镜和 HSL。点“导出”后，画面会显示“正在导出”和百分比进度；完成后可到“照片”的最近项目中查找 MP4。

若设备无法实时合成视频滤镜，应用会提示并显示原视频；导出仍会尝试应用当前编辑参数。以完成后的导出文件为准。遇到失败时，请记录提示内容，避免连续重复导出占满存储。

配方、数据与换设备

在“配方”中输入名称并点“保存配方”。点击列表中的配方可应用它；选择需要的配方后点“批量应用”，再选择照片。点击删除时会先出现“删除这个配方？”确认框：确认“删除”才会移除该条配方，点“取消”可保留。

配方保存于当前设备，Luma 没有账号或开发者提供的云同步。删除 App 会移除应用内数据；已经导出到照片图库的作品会保留。系统备份是否恢复配方取决于你的 Apple 备份设置。排查问题前请先保留需要的作品与配方参数。

界面、语言与 iPhone Duo

界面提供简体中文和英语，跟随系统语言设置；也可在系统的 App 语言设置中选择。iPhone Duo 的展开和半折状态会调整预览与工具的排列。若布局异常，请附上设备状态、方向和截图，帮助我们定位。

联系 Gnod Studio

请发送邮件至 gnodstudio@gmail.com，说明 App 版本与构建号、设备型号、iOS 版本、素材格式、操作步骤及实际错误提示。截图或不含私人内容的示例文件有助于复现。

我们不会索取密码或付款信息。无需发送完整私人相册；提供最小可复现示例即可。支持资料的处理方式见隐私政策。

LUMA / Help & support

Keep creating.

Luma supports iOS 17 or later and is being prepared for the App Store. This page does not offer an App Store download or a public beta invitation.

From media to a finished edit

After importing a photo or RAW file, open Presets, choose an effect from its live thumbnail, and adjust the filter strength. Undo in the top bar steps back through recent edits; hold the preview to compare with the original. Open Recipes to save a look for another edit or a batch.

Crop and HSL adjustments

Open Crop and drag the crop frame or its handles in the preview to change the composition. Combine this with aspect ratio, rotation, flip, and perspective controls. In HSL, choose a color channel, then hue, saturation, or lightness. Reset Channel resets only the selected color channel.

On an expanded iPhone Duo, basic adjustments are grouped, the eight HSL color channels form a grid, and saved recipes appear in a vertical list.

Photo access and missing media

Check access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Luma. With limited access, the app can show only the media you allowed. Adjust your selection in system settings.

If a file is stored only in iCloud, open it in Photos and wait for the download before importing it in Luma. The current picker shows up to the 120 most recent matching items. For older photos or RAW files, save a copy to Files and use the file-import option.

RAW and video compatibility

Common photos, DNG RAW files, and MP4 video are supported. Camera RAW formats, codecs, and video specifications depend on your device and system decoder. RAW edits use a lightweight preview and decode the original again at export. The output is an edited image, not a rewritten RAW original.

Large RAW files, long clips, and high-resolution video need more memory, free storage, and time. Leave enough space and keep the app in the foreground during export. If a file fails to import, try another file of the same type and tell support its format and your device details.

Exports and previews

Check that Luma can add to Photos, then tap Export. After a photo is saved, the message offers Share and Keep Editing. Share opens the system share sheet so you can choose an app or contact to receive the photo. Original media is not overwritten.

For video, set the in and out points, speed, mute, filters, and HSL. Tap Export to see the Exporting message and percentage progress. Find the completed MP4 in your recent photos.

If the device cannot render video filters in real time, Luma explains the fallback and shows the original video; export still attempts to apply the current edit. Check the completed output file. If an export fails, record the message and avoid repeatedly exporting until storage is full.

Recipes, local data, and moving devices

Enter a name in Recipes and tap Save Recipe. Select a saved recipe to apply it; choose the recipe you want, then tap Batch Apply and select photos. Deleting an item first opens Delete this recipe?: choose Delete to remove it or Cancel to keep it.

Recipes are stored on your current device. Luma has no account or developer-provided cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local data; exports already in Photos remain. Whether an Apple backup restores recipes depends on your backup settings. Keep copies of important work and recipe values before troubleshooting.

Language, layout, and iPhone Duo

The app supports English and Simplified Chinese and follows system language settings. You can also use the per-app language setting where available. Opening or partially folding iPhone Duo changes the preview and tool layout. If something looks wrong, send the device state, orientation, and a screenshot.
