From understanding to practice.

Keep exercises, plans and records connected.

01 / LEARN

One exercise, step by step.

Every exercise includes three key-pose illustrations, setup, breathing, common mistakes and adjustments. Choose a male or female demonstrator and explore the steps at your own pace. Find exercises by muscle group, equipment or name.

02 / PLAN

Make room in your week.

During setup, choose your demonstrator, training focus and weekly schedule, then preview and save. Start with a two-, three- or four-day home or gym template and customize exercise order, sets, targets and rest.

03 / PRACTICE

Give this set your attention.

Log weight, reps or duration; distinguish warm-up and working sets; and track each side separately for unilateral exercises. On the phone, three-step illustrations follow the same timed rep cycle. Check your actual reps before confirming the set. Supports kg/lb, rest timers and resuming unfinished workouts.

On Apple Watch, see the current exercise, automatically cycle through its steps and view the phone's rep count and status. Stay connected to the paired iPhone to adjust and confirm a set; the phone saves your training records.

04 / REFLECT

See progress in your own records.

Review workout history and recent weekly frequency, add notes and correct entries. Compare performance for the same exercise, equipment, side and weight. Export CSV or restore from a JSON backup.

About content and data
Core phone features work offline and training stays on your device. There are no accounts, ads or developer analytics. Watch communicates with the paired iPhone; offline it can only show cached information, not record sets independently. Step illustrations show static key poses, not continuous video. Rep guidance is time-based, does not use sensors to detect actual movements and never completes a set automatically. XUNLI provides general fitness information and logging, not a replacement for medical advice or professional instruction. It has no automatic cloud sync, Apple Health access, heart-rate or calorie tracking.
