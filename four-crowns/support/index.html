《四冠：王国牌局》· iPhone 与 iPad

怎么玩

黑桃、方块、红桃与梅花分别维系军队、金库、民心与粮仓。从手牌中选择 1–5 张牌，再出牌结算牌型、倍率和各国柱收益。让四柱都维持在零以上，撑过每一场危机。

点按卡牌可选择或取消；界面会预览当前牌型与收益。设置页可切换语言与调整音乐、音效音量。

存档与联网

进度与设置自动保存在当前设备。本游戏没有账号、跨设备云存档或在线服务。删除应用也会删除本地进度。

牌型不等于赌博

游戏借用对子、顺子、同花等传统扑克牌型进行策略计算，但没有投注、赌场玩法、真实货币、可兑换奖励、开箱或应用内购买。

语言与设备

支持简体中文、英语和日语；为 iPhone 与 iPad 横屏触控设计。

Support

Four Crowns · iPhone and iPad

How to play

Spades, Diamonds, Hearts, and Clubs sustain your Army, Treasury, Popular Support, and Granary. Select one to five cards, then play the hand to resolve its poker rank, multiplier, and pillar gains. Keep every pillar above zero through each crisis.

Save data and connectivity

Progress and settings are saved automatically on the current device. There are no accounts, cross-device cloud saves, or online services. Deleting the app also deletes local progress.

No gambling

Four Crowns uses familiar poker ranks for strategy calculations. It contains no betting, casino play, real money, redeemable prizes, loot boxes, or in-app purchases.

Languages and devices

Simplified Chinese, English, and Japanese are supported. The game is designed for landscape touch play on iPhone and iPad.
