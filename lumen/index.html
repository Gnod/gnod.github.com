LUMEN · BY GNOD STUDIO

A small light.
A world to untangle.

LUMEN is a minimal line-puzzle game across six worlds and 129 rooms. Read the rhythm of patrols, connect switches and gates, and discover optional paths. No accounts, ads or in-app purchases. Progress stays on your device; the iOS app works offline.

Use arrow keys or WASD on desktop, or swipe on a touchscreen. Play in landscape. Release to stop on normal rails. Press Escape or tap the top-right corner to pause.
