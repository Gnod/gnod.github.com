Privacy policy

Your photo editing happens on your device.

Effective November 10, 2026. This policy covers the YouMark iOS app from Gnod Studio and these YouMark website pages.

What the app processes

YouMark does not require an account. It makes local copies of photos you select, reads available photo metadata, and renders watermarks on your device. For supported Live Photos, it also stores the paired video. Metadata can include camera and lens details, exposure settings, capture dates, image dimensions, and GPS coordinates already embedded in a photo.

Your imported media, metadata, custom text, and editing choices are not automatically uploaded to Gnod Studio. The app has no advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not request your current device location.

Local storage and preferences

Imported files and photo-editing records are kept in the app's storage. Device preferences include appearance, language, onboarding status, favorite templates, recent templates for each photo, and template settings such as icons, formatting, and custom field text. These let you reopen photos and continue editing.

YouMark has no account-based cloud sync operated by Gnod Studio. App data may be included in device backups or transfers according to your Apple settings. Gnod Studio does not operate or control those backups.

Photos permissions and Apple services

You choose what to import through Apple's photo picker. Saving a result requests permission to add images to your photo library. You can manage Photos access for YouMark in system Settings; denying save permission does not send photos to us.

Local editing is separate from system services. Retrieving an iCloud-only photo, iCloud Photos synchronization, device backup, App Store downloads or ratings, and system diagnostics may involve Apple services and network access under your settings. Those services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Saving and sharing

Saving creates a new photo-library item; it does not replace the original photo. If you use the system share sheet, the destination you choose receives the shared result and handles it under its own policies. YouMark does not automatically post your photos.

Watermark text becomes part of the exported image. Check it for private dates, locations, names, or other sensitive details before saving or sharing. Hiding a displayed field is not a metadata-removal tool and does not remove information from the imported original. Do not rely on YouMark as a way to strip all metadata.

Removing data

Deleting a photo from YouMark removes its imported copy, paired video if present, and photo-editing record after the temporary Undo option ends. Other app preferences, such as favorites and template settings, including stored custom field text, can remain.

This does not delete originals or exported images in Photos, files shared elsewhere, or backup copies. Manage those in Photos, the receiving app, or your backup settings. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data; offloading the app is different and can retain data. Restoring a backup can restore earlier app data. We cannot recover deleted local edits for you.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer and troubleshoot your request, not for advertising. Email is handled by the email services involved, separately from local photo editing.

Send only what is needed for support. Please redact private details and do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary original photos. Contact us at gnodstudio@gmail.com with questions or a request to delete support correspondence. You do not need to send us your photo library to remove local app data.

These website pages

The YouMark pages are static information pages. They have no photo uploads, contact form, advertising, analytics, remote fonts, or tracking cookies. The language selector uses a URL parameter without writing cookies or browser storage.

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to serve and protect the site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following an App Store or other external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Gnod Studio at gnodstudio@gmail.com. We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change.

隐私政策

照片编辑在你的设备上进行。

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日。本政策适用于 Gnod Studio 的 YouMark iOS 应用及这些 YouMark 网站页面。

应用处理哪些信息

YouMark 不需要注册账号。应用为你选择的照片创建本地副本，读取其中已有的照片元数据，并在设备上渲染水印。对于支持的实况照片，还会保存配对视频。元数据可能包含相机、镜头、曝光参数、拍摄时间、图片尺寸，以及照片中已有的 GPS 坐标。

导入的媒体、元数据、自定义文字和编辑选项不会自动上传给 Gnod Studio。应用不含广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方分析 SDK，也不请求设备的实时位置。

本地存储与偏好

导入文件和照片编辑记录保存在应用的存储空间中。本地偏好包括外观、语言、引导页状态、收藏模板、每张照片最近使用的模板，以及图标、格式、自定义字段文字等模板设置，用于重新打开照片和继续编辑。

YouMark 不提供由 Gnod Studio 运营的账号云同步。根据你的 Apple 设置，应用数据可能包含在设备备份或迁移中；Gnod Studio 不运营或控制这些备份。

照片权限与 Apple 服务

你通过 Apple 的照片选择器决定导入哪些照片。保存成片时，应用会请求向照片图库添加图片的权限。你可以在系统设置中管理 YouMark 的照片访问权限；拒绝保存权限不会把照片发送给我们。

本地编辑与系统服务是不同的环节。下载仅在 iCloud 中的照片、iCloud 照片同步、设备备份、App Store 下载或评分以及系统诊断，可能根据你的设置使用 Apple 服务和网络。这些服务遵循 Apple 隐私政策。

保存与分享

保存会在照片图库中创建新项目，不会覆盖原图。使用系统分享面板时，你选择的目标应用或服务会收到分享结果，并按照其自身政策处理。YouMark 不会自动发布你的照片。

水印文字会成为导出图片的一部分。保存或分享前，请检查是否包含私人日期、位置、姓名或其他敏感内容。隐藏显示字段不等于清除元数据，也不会移除导入原图中的信息。请不要将 YouMark 当作彻底清除元数据的工具。

删除数据

在 YouMark 中删除照片后，短暂的“撤销”机会结束时，应用会移除该照片的导入副本、配对视频（如有）及照片编辑记录。收藏、模板设置等其他应用偏好可能继续保留，其中可能包括已保存的自定义字段文字。

这不会删除系统“照片”中的原图或已导出的图片、已分享至其他地方的文件，也不会删除备份副本。这些内容需要在“照片”、接收内容的应用或备份设置中管理。删除应用会移除设备上的应用数据；系统“卸载 App”与删除不同，可能保留数据。恢复备份也可能带回之前的应用数据。我们无法替你恢复已删除的本地编辑记录。

支持邮件

当你主动发送邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、正文及你选择发送的附件。这些信息用于回复和排查问题，不用于广告。邮件由相关邮件服务处理，与本地照片编辑是不同的流程。

请只发送支持所需的信息，遮挡私人内容，不要发送密码、付款资料或不必要的原始照片。如有隐私疑问或需要删除支持往来邮件，请联系 gnodstudio@gmail.com。清除本地应用数据不需要把图库发送给我们。

这些网站页面

YouMark 页面仅提供静态信息，不提供照片上传或联系表单，不包含广告、分析代码、远程字体或跟踪 Cookie。语言切换仅使用网址参数，不写入 Cookie 或浏览器存储。

这些页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。托管方可能处理 IP 地址、请求日志等技术信息，以提供并保护网站服务，详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。打开 App Store 或其他外部链接后，相应服务适用其自身的隐私规则。

联系与更新

隐私问题请通过 gnodstudio@gmail.com 联系 Gnod Studio。处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本页和生效日期。
