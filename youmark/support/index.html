How do I start?

Tap Add Photos, choose a photo in the system picker, then open it in YouMark. Choose a template and adjust the fields, colors, and text. You can revisit the introduction in the app's Settings.

A photo will not import

Open it in Apple's Photos app first and wait for the original to load, especially if it is stored only in iCloud. That download can need a network connection. Check available device storage, then try importing again. If only one photo fails, try a different original photo and report the file type and error message.

Why are camera details missing or incorrect?

YouMark can only read metadata present in the imported file. Screenshots, downloaded pictures, or copies sent through another app may lack camera, lens, date, or exposure information. Use the original file when possible and check Photo Info in YouMark.

In a template's field controls, select another available source, enter custom text, or hide a field you do not want to display. A custom value changes the watermark text, not the original camera metadata. Hiding a field does not sanitize the source photo.

How do I save or share?

Use the save menu for a preview-size image or an original-size render. The result is a new item in Photos, not a replacement for your original. Use Share to send a rendered image through the system share sheet to a destination you choose.

If saving is denied, open system Settings, find YouMark's Photos permissions, and allow adding photos. If saving still fails, check free storage and try a preview-size export. For several photos, use the selection controls on the photo list and then export.

What about Live Photos and HDR?

The Live Photo save option appears when an imported photo has its paired video available. If the video could not be imported, try the original Live Photo from Photos again. HDR preservation depends on the source image and a compatible template; some exports are ordinary still images or SDR. Regular image sharing is not a Live Photo export.

What does deleting a photo remove?

Use the photo list's deletion controls to remove a local YouMark copy and its edit record. Tap Undo while it is offered if the removal was accidental. Once deletion is finalized, YouMark cannot restore that edit record for you.

Your original and saved exports in Photos are not deleted. Remove them in Photos if needed. Favorites and other app preferences, including custom text saved in template settings, can remain after deleting an item. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: it can remove local photos and edits. Offloading an app can retain data, and restoring a device backup may bring older data back.

Templates, language, and appearance

Each photo remembers its selected and recently used templates. Favorite templates and template icon choices are app preferences. Templates may use a corresponding portrait or landscape layout for the current photo. Choose light, dark, or system appearance and English, Simplified Chinese, or system language in Settings.

A layout or touch control looks wrong

Note the app version in Settings and your iOS version. On iPhone Duo, include whether you were using the outer display, open inner display, or a partially folded posture, plus the orientation and any split-window arrangement. On iPad, include the window size or multitasking mode. Reopen the affected page and check whether the issue repeats.

Email the steps to reproduce it and, if helpful, a redacted screenshot. Please do not erase local data just to test a layout problem.

Send a useful support request

  • Device model, iOS version, and YouMark version from Settings.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps that reproduce it.
  • The template and photo type involved, such as JPEG, HEIC, or Live Photo.
  • An error message or redacted screenshot, only if necessary.

Email gnodstudio@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, payment details, or private original photos unless they are truly needed and you choose to share them. See our privacy policy for how local data and support messages are handled.

如何开始？

点击“添加照片”，在系统选择器中选择照片，再从 YouMark 打开它。选择模板后，可以调整字段、颜色和文字。应用“设置”中也可以重新查看引导页。

照片无法导入

先在系统“照片”中打开它，等待原图加载完成，尤其是仅保存在 iCloud 中的照片。这个下载过程可能需要联网。检查设备剩余空间后再尝试导入。如果只有某张照片失败，请用另一张原图对照，并在反馈时注明文件类型和错误信息。

为什么拍摄参数缺失或不正确？

YouMark 只能读取导入文件中已有的元数据。截图、下载的图片或经其他应用转发的副本，可能没有相机、镜头、日期或曝光信息。尽量使用原图，并在 YouMark 的“照片信息”中检查。

在模板的字段编辑中，可以切换为其他可用参数、输入自定义文字，或隐藏不想显示的字段。自定义内容改变的是水印文字，不是原始拍摄元数据；隐藏字段也不会清除原图里的敏感信息。

如何保存或分享？

保存菜单提供预览尺寸和原图尺寸渲染。成片会作为新项目加入系统“照片”，不会替换原图。“分享”通过系统分享面板，将渲染后的图片发送到你选择的目标。

保存被拒绝时，请在系统设置中找到 YouMark 的照片权限，允许添加照片。如果仍然失败，检查存储空间，并尝试导出预览尺寸。多张照片可以在列表中选择后批量导出。

实况照片和 HDR 有什么限制？

导入的实况照片包含可用的配对视频时，才会显示实况照片保存选项。如果视频未成功导入，可以从系统“照片”重新导入原始实况照片。保留 HDR 取决于原图和模板是否兼容；部分导出结果是普通静态图片或 SDR。常规图片分享不等同于实况照片导出。

删除照片会删除哪些内容？

使用照片列表中的删除操作，移除 YouMark 内的本地副本和编辑记录。误删后，可以在短暂显示的提示中点击“撤销”。删除正式完成后，YouMark 无法替你恢复该编辑记录。

系统“照片”中的原图和已保存成片不会被删除。如需删除这些内容，请在“照片”中操作。删除某张照片后，收藏及其他应用偏好可能仍然保留，包括模板设置中保存的自定义文字。不要把删除或重装应用作为首个排查步骤，这可能导致本地照片和编辑记录丢失。系统“卸载 App”可能保留数据，恢复设备备份也可能带回旧数据。

模板、语言与外观

每张照片分别记忆选中的模板和最近使用记录；收藏模板和模板图标则属于应用偏好。模板可能根据当前照片对应为横向或竖向布局。你可以在设置中选择浅色、深色或跟随系统外观，以及英文、简体中文或跟随系统语言。

布局或点击区域不正常

先记录设置中的应用版本和 iOS 版本。iPhone Duo 请说明使用的是外屏、展开内屏还是半折状态，以及屏幕方向和是否分屏；iPad 请注明窗口大小或多任务模式。重新打开相关页面，检查问题是否重复出现。

请邮件发送复现步骤；有需要时可以附上遮挡私人信息的截图。不要为了验证布局问题而清空本地数据。

反馈时提供哪些信息？

  • 设备型号、iOS 版本，以及设置中的 YouMark 版本。
  • 期望结果、实际情况和可复现的操作步骤。
  • 相关模板和照片类型，例如 JPEG、HEIC 或实况照片。
  • 必要时提供错误信息或已遮挡隐私的截图。

请发送至 gnodstudio@gmail.com。不要发送密码、付款信息；除非确有必要且你愿意提供，也不要发送私人原始照片。本地数据与支持邮件的处理方式见 隐私政策。
